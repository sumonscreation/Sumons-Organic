About Our Agriculture

Shaping a Healthier Planet Through Home Agriculture

At Sumons, our mission is to reconnect people with the food they eat and the earth it grows from. We aim to make agriculture accessible, joyful, and part of everyday life — starting right at home. By turning rooftops, balconies, and backyards into thriving gardens, we empower families to grow fresh, healthy food and live in harmony with nature.

Sumons’s mission is to make the act of growing food a lifestyle, not a luxury. Through education, innovation, & a deep respect for nature, we help individuals build healthier environments, reduce their carbon footprint, and cultivate independence one garden at a time.

Our Vision

Leading The Movement Toward Regenerative Living

Our vision is simple yet powerful: we want to create a world where sustainable farming is not just a choice, but a way of life. Through innovation, education, and community, Sumons strives to make farming accessible to everyone — helping people grow food at home, nourish their bodies, and build a greener planet for future generations.

Our vision is bold but clear: reconnect people with nature and food through innovation, design, and community. We see a world where clean food is grown close to home, where people find peace and purpose in planting, and where sustainability is not a trend — but a way of life.
